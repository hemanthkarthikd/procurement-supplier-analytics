# Procurement & Supplier Performance Analytics

An end-to-end procurement analytics project using **SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI** to evaluate supplier performance, procurement spend, delivery reliability, quality, cost variance, and sourcing risk.

## Business Problem

A mid-sized manufacturing company purchases materials and components from a network of domestic and international suppliers.

Procurement leadership wants better visibility into supplier performance and spending patterns in order to identify:

- high-spend suppliers and categories
- late-delivery risk
- supplier quality issues
- purchase-price variance
- supplier concentration risk
- potential cost-saving opportunities
- candidates for supplier consolidation or diversification

The objective of this project is to transform transactional procurement data into actionable sourcing and supplier-management insights.

## Key Business Questions

1. Which suppliers and categories account for the largest share of procurement spend?
2. Which suppliers have the strongest and weakest delivery performance?
3. Which suppliers have the highest defect rates?
4. How much do actual purchase prices vary from standard or expected prices?
5. Which suppliers have highly variable lead times?
6. Where is the company overly dependent on a small number of suppliers?
7. Which suppliers combine high spend with poor operational performance?
8. What sourcing actions could reduce cost and supply risk?

## Planned Analysis

- Procurement spend analysis
- ABC / Pareto supplier classification
- Supplier OTIF analysis
- Lead-time analysis
- Purchase-price variance analysis
- Quality and defect analysis
- Supplier concentration analysis
- Supplier performance scorecard
- Supplier risk segmentation
- Savings opportunity identification

## Tools

- **SQL** — querying, KPI calculation, spend and supplier analysis
- **Python / Pandas** — data cleaning, exploratory analysis, scoring, and validation
- **Excel** — supplementary procurement analysis and scenario modeling
- **Power BI** — interactive supplier and procurement dashboard

## Dataset

A realistic **synthetic procurement dataset** will be created for this portfolio project. It will simulate purchase-order transactions across suppliers, material categories, regions, delivery performance, pricing, and quality metrics.
